## Quotes and Definitions

- **Method validation:** "confirmation through objective evidence that the requirements for a specific intended use can be fulfilled" (ISO/IEC 17025:2017 core requirement)
- **Measurement uncertainty:** "parameter characterizing the dispersion of values that could reasonably be attributed to the measured quantity" (aligned with JCGM 100)
- **Decision rule:** "rule describing how a measurement result is used to determine conformity with a specified requirement"
- **Statement of conformity:** "documented declaration issued by a laboratory confirming the results and uncertainties in relation to specified requirements"
- **Measurement result:** "set of values attributed to a measurand with all available information about uncertainty and traceability"
- **Clause 6.5:** Evaluation of measurement uncertainty; guidance on responsibility for measurement traceability
- **Clause 7.6:** Evaluation of conformity; requirement for documented decision rules
- **Clause 7.7:** Statement of conformity; requirement to define rules, include measurement uncertainty when specified
