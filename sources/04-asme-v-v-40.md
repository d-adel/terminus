## Quotes and Concepts

- **Question of interest:** Primary question the computational model is being used to answer; initial requirement specification
- **Context of use:** Operational envelope and scenarios for which the model is being validated
- **Model risk:** Assessment of how model uncertainties and limitations affect decision quality
- **Model influence:** Degree to which model outputs affect product performance and safety decisions
- **Decision consequence:** Impact if the model provides incorrect answers
- **Credibility factors:** Evidence sources demonstrating model fitness for purpose (benchmarking, sensitivity analysis, uncertainty quantification)
- **Credibility goals:** Target levels of evidence required for acceptable risk
- **Applicability:** Whether model applies to the intended use case (related to validation domain vs application domain)
