# Terminus Survey Index

Collected primary-source material for Terminus design discussion on conformity assessment, validation evidence, and qualification schemes.

## Files

- **01-iso-17000.md** - ISO/IEC 17000:2020 conformity assessment vocabulary (paywalled, secondary sources used)
- **02-iso-17025.md** - ISO/IEC 17025:2017 method validation and statements of conformity (paywalled, secondary used)
- **03-ilac-g8.md** - ILAC-G8:09/2019 decision rules and statements of conformity (public PDF)
- **04-asme-v-v-40.md** - ASME V&V 40-2018 and FDA guidance on computational modeling (paywalled, secondary used)
- **05-nasa-std-7009.md** - NASA-STD-7009 models and simulations credibility assessment (paywalled, secondary used)
- **06-oberkampf-trucano.md** - Oberkampf & Trucano / Oberkampf & Roy on validation domains and PCMM (paywalled, secondary used)
- **07-gsn.md** - Goal Structuring Notation v3 (2021) element types (secondary sources used)
- **08-in-toto.md** - in-toto Attestation Framework statement fields and SLSA integration (public GitHub)
- **09-w3c-prov.md** - W3C PROV-O core classes and relations (public standard)
- **10-vim.md** - JCGM 200:2012 International Vocabulary of Metrology and JCGM 100:2008 GUM (public references)

## Summary

- **Primary sources fully accessible:** PROV-O, SLSA, in-toto, ILAC-G8
- **Secondary sources consulted:** ISO standards, ASME, NASA, Oberkampf papers, GSN, VIM
- **All ISO standards paywalled:** Used Wikipedia, regulatory summaries, and official descriptions
- **JCGM documents:** Public references via BIPM; definitions extracted from Wikipedia article on JCGM
