# JCGM 200:2012 International Vocabulary of Metrology (VIM) and JCGM 100:2008 GUM

**Title 1:** JCGM 200:2012 International Vocabulary of Metrology — Basic and General Concepts and Associated Terms

**Title 2:** JCGM 100:2008 (GUM) Evaluation of Measurement Data — Guide to the Expression of Uncertainty in Measurement

**URL:** https://www.bipm.org/documents/ (full PDFs; public BIPM repository)

**Version/Date:** JCGM 200:2012 (VIM); JCGM 100:2008 (GUM)

**Access:** Public PDFs available from BIPM (Bureau International des Poids et Mesures); no paywall.

**Source(s):** BIPM official publication repository; Wikipedia article on JCGM and measurement metrology.

## JCGM 200:2012 (VIM) Definitions

- **Measurand:** "Quantity intended to be measured; a property or characteristic that can have a numerical value assigned"
- **Measurement method:** "Generic description of a logical organization of operations used in a measurement"
- **Measurement procedure:** "Detailed description of a measurement; set of operations that must be carried out to measure a particular measurand in a particular way"
- **Measurement result:** "Set of values attributed to a measured quantity together with all available information describing the reliability of the values"
- **Measured quantity value:** "Value of a quantity representing a measurement result"
- **Measurement uncertainty:** "Non-negative parameter characterizing the dispersion of the values that could reasonably be attributed to the measurand"
- **Reference measurement procedure:** "Measurement procedure that is used to calibrate or validate another measurement procedure, or as the basis for comparison with a tested procedure"
- **Metrological traceability:** "Property of a measurement result whereby the result can be related to a reference through a documented unbroken chain of calibrations, each contributing to the measurement uncertainty"

## JCGM 100:2008 (GUM) Concepts

- **Title:** "Evaluation of Measurement Data — Guide to the Expression of Uncertainty in Measurement"
- **Purpose:** Establishes rules for evaluating and expressing measurement uncertainty; replaces traditional error analysis with probabilistic approach
- **Core principle:** "A measurement result is complete only when it is accompanied by a statement of the associated uncertainty"
- **Standard uncertainty:** Standard deviation derived from a state-of-knowledge probability distribution representing possible values of the measured quantity
- **Type A evaluation:** Uncertainty evaluation based on repeated measured values, typically assuming Gaussian distributions
- **Type B evaluation:** Uncertainty evaluation founded on scientific judgment and available information when repeated measurements are not feasible
- **Law of propagation of uncertainty:** Method for combining individual uncertainties through their squared values in indirect measurements

## Notes

Both JCGM documents are maintained by BIPM and are free public documents. JCGM 200:2012 (VIM) is the authoritative source for metrology terminology. JCGM 100:2008 (GUM) is widely recognized and adopted by major National Measurement Institutes and integrated into ISO/IEC 17025.
