## Concepts (From Secondary Sources)

- **Models and simulations credibility:** Assessment of computational model fitness for decision-making and operational use
- **Credibility assessment:** Documented evaluation process examining model validation, verification, and uncertainty quantification
- **Domain of validity / validation domain:** Bounded region of input space and conditions for which the model has been demonstrated to be reliable
- **Use outside validated domain:** Extrapolation or application beyond demonstration boundaries; requires additional justification or risk assessment
- **Applicability:** Whether a credible model applies to a new operational scenario
