## Concepts (From Secondary References)

- **Validation domain:** Bounded region of input parameter space and physical conditions for which a computational model has been demonstrated valid through comparison to experimental data
- **Application domain:** Operational region where the model will be used; may partially or entirely exceed validation domain boundaries
- **Extrapolation:** Use of a validated model outside its demonstrated validation domain; requires explicit uncertainty assumptions or additional verification
- **Predictive capability maturity:** Structured assessment of model credibility from low (unvalidated) to high (fully validated across application envelope)
- **PCMM levels:** Progression through maturity stages based on evidence (benchmarking, sensitivity analysis, uncertainty propagation)
