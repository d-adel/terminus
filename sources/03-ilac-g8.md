# ILAC-G8:09/2019 Guidelines on Decision Rules and Statements of Conformity

**Title:** ILAC Guidelines on Decision Rules and Statements of Conformity

**URL:** https://ilac.org/publications-and-resources/ilac-guidance-series/

**Version/Date:** ILAC-G8:09/2019 (September 2019)

**Access:** Public PDF (free download from ILAC website)

**Source:** ILAC (International Laboratory Accreditation Cooperation) official publications.

## Document Purpose and Scope

- **Purpose:** "to assist laboratories in applying decision rules when issuing statements of conformity to a specification or standard as required by ISO/IEC 17025:2017"
- **Audience:** Laboratories, assessors, regulators, and customers evaluating decision rules and conformity requirements
- **Revision:** "significantly revised to assist laboratories in the use of decision rules when issuing statements of conformity to a specification or standard as required in the 2017 edition of ISO/IEC 17025"

## Key Concepts (As Described)

- **Binary vs non-binary decision rules:** Guidance distinguishes between pass/fail decisions and conditional assessments
- **Guard bands:** Coverage of how measurement uncertainty affects conformity statements
- **Statements of conformity:** Defines accepted statement statuses and their meanings

## Quotes

- Document describes the "functional approach" to conformity assessment: "selection, determination, review, decision, attestation"
- Covers "statement categories" used to communicate results to stakeholders
