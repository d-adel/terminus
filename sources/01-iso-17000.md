## Quotes and Definitions

- **Conformity assessment:** "determination whether an object of conformity assessment fulfils specified requirements" (ISO/IEC 17000 core definition)
- **Object of conformity assessment:** "product, process, service, person, body, or system" subject to assessment
- **Specified requirement:** "requirement to which an object of conformity assessment must conform; may be expressed in standards, regulations, or contracts"
- **Statement of conformity:** "documented decision on conformity; issued by a conformity assessment body indicating whether an object meets specified requirements"
- **Attestation:** "provision of a statement, based on a decision that a specified requirement has been fulfilled"
- **Conformity assessment scheme:** "set of rules, procedures, and management for carrying out conformity assessment activities; administered by a scheme owner"
- **Scheme owner:** "body responsible for establishing, maintaining, and operating a conformity assessment scheme"
